Frequently Asked Questions

How can I start learning vocabulary?

Select any lesson button. The words of that lesson will appear as cards.

How can I listen to a word's pronunciation?

Click the speaker icon on a vocabulary card.

Where can I see word details?

Click the information icon on a card to open the details modal.

How can I search for a word?

Type a word in the search box. Matching words and meanings will appear.
